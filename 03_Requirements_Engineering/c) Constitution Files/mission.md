# Mission Statement: Identity Entropy Engine

## Core Purpose
The **Identity Entropy Engine (IEE)** is a conceptual software project designed to counteract digital profiling by introducing high-entropy noise into search engine algorithms. Its primary objective is to "poison" user profiles by executing arbitrary, unpredictable search queries that dilute coherent behavioral patterns.

## Strategic Goals
1.  **Obfuscation:** Prevent search engines from constructing accurate interest profiles by flooding query histories with semantically unrelated terms.
2.  **Evasion:** Mimic human-like irregularity in search timing and interaction to avoid detection as an automated bot.
3.  **Autonomy:** Operate as a self-sufficient local application, requiring minimal user intervention once configured.

## Operational Principles
- **Randomness First:** All query selection is strictly random from a predefined static dictionary, ensuring no semantic bias.
- **Temporal Variance:** Search intervals vary arbitrarily between 2 minutes and 2 hours to simulate sporadic human activity.
- **Local Execution:** The engine runs entirely on the user's machine, ensuring privacy and control over the generated data.
- **Iterative Evolution:** The system evolves through three distinct lifecycle phases, increasing sophistication in automation and evasion techniques.

## Ethical & Legal Disclaimer
*This project is a theoretical mock-up intended for educational and research purposes regarding privacy mechanisms and search engine behavior. It is not intended for malicious deployment or violation of third-party Terms of Service.*
